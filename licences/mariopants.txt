Mariopants 1.1, by Brad Smith (rainwarrior), http://rainwarrior.ca, released
17 December 2013.

bbmp-retro's "shroom" engine takes the Mario Paint song format and the way of
playing it from Mariopants' files.cpp and player.cpp, written again for the player
(songs end at their last note); its note recordings are made at build time from
Mariopants' data/notes.wav. Its terms (source.txt):

  This source code is released without any standardized license.
  The user is permitted to modify, reuse and redistribute it as needed,
  but please try to attribute its original author and mark changes,
  so that it is not misrepresented.
  
  The included data files are presumed fair to use, but they are, for the most part,
  extracted from the original Mario Paint game. This author does not claim copyright
  or ownership of these data files.

This is that credit; the changes are marked in csrc/retro/shroom_engine.cpp.
